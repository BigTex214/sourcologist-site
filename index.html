About Sourcologist

Sourcologist is an independent healthcare recruiting practice founded on a simple conviction: the best clinicians aren't on LinkedIn, and the best placements don't come from job boards. After a decade of placing physicians, CRNAs, and advanced practice providers across the country, I've built Sourcologist around a single principle — finding the right clinician for the right role, faster than anyone else. No algorithms replacing judgment. No databases replacing relationships. Just precise, specialty-aware sourcing from a recruiter who actually answers the phone.

— Bo Ehmke, Founder
